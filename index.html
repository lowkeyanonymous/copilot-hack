BITCOIN TO CASH APP
Possible route—not a transfer feature

            
If you're mining with your own ASIC, a pool payout that actually reaches a wallet you control could fund a later transfer. Pool payout thresholds and timing vary, so mining does not guarantee money each month. This dashboard cannot send money or connect to your pool or Cash App. Whether you can receive BTC in Cash App depends on your account's current eligibility and the pool/provider's payout options.

            
    
              
  1. Check Cash App's current Bitcoin deposit availability, limits, and supported network in the official app.
    2. 
              
  2. If eligible, copy a fresh Bitcoin receiving address from Cash App and check that the provider supports withdrawals to that address on the same network. A $Cashtag is not a Bitcoin address.
    3. 
              
  3. Send only a small test amount first if you proceed. Blockchain transfers are generally irreversible; provider minimums, network fees, delays, and account checks may apply.
    4. 
              
  4. To get dollars, a received BTC balance may need to be sold in Cash App; availability, fees, spread, identity checks, and tax rules can apply. Bitcoin's value can fall before or after a payout.
    5. 
            

            
Never give a provider your Cash App password, sign-in code, wallet recovery phrase, or private key. Ignore anyone promising guaranteed daily returns or demanding extra money to “unlock” a withdrawal.

FROM ESTIMATE TO SETUP

              
What you need to mine

            

            NO WALLET SECRETS OR POOL CREDENTIALS STORED HERE
          

          

            

              01
              
Get a SHA-256 ASIC
Bitcoin proof-of-work needs purpose-built ASIC hardware. A regular PC or graphics card is not a practical Bitcoin miner.

            

            

              02
              
Plan power & cooling
Check your circuit, local electricity price, ventilation, noise, and heat before running a miner. Operating costs can exceed payouts.

            

            

              03
              
Choose a mining pool
Most miners join a pool for more regular payouts. Compare its payout method, fee, minimum payout, and reputation.

            

            

              04
              
Configure on the ASIC
Enter the pool’s URL, worker name, and payout details in your miner’s own trusted admin interface. Never enter a seed phrase here.

            

          

          
This tool does not connect to miner admin pages, wallets, or pool accounts. Use only equipment and electricity you are authorized to operate.

        

        
Bitcoin mining is competitive and outcomes are uncertain. An estimate is not a promise of earnings.
